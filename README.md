## 🏆 Real free domains (you actually register the name)

### 1. GitHub Student Developer Pack
**Link:** https://education.github.com/pack
**You get:** A free **.me** domain via Namecheap (1 year), one free domain via **Name.com** (choose from 25+ extensions), and a free **.tech** domain (1 year).
**Catch:** Students only — you need to verify student status. Renewals after year 1 are paid.

### 2. EU.org
**Link:** https://nic.eu.org
**You get:** A free `yourname.eu.org` domain — running since **1996**, free forever, full DNS control.
**Catch:** Every application is reviewed manually, and approval can take **weeks to months**. Not for anything urgent.

### 3. PP.UA
**Link:** https://pp.ua
**You get:** A free `.pp.ua` domain (Ukraine's personal-use TLD) that behaves like a real registered domain.
**Catch:** You must activate/re-confirm it periodically (free, via their bot/SMS) or it gets deleted. Intended for personal, non-commercial use.

## 💻 Free subdomains for developers (work like real domains)

### 4. is-a.dev
**Link:** https://www.is-a.dev
**You get:** `yourname.is-a.dev` — free forever. Register by opening a pull request on `github.com/is-a-dev/register` with a small JSON file. Supports A, CNAME, MX, TXT records. It's on the Public Suffix List, so platforms treat it like a real domain.
**Catch:** For developer/personal projects only; your site must be live before the PR is approved.

### 5. JS.ORG
**Link:** https://js.org
**You get:** `yourname.js.org` — free forever, also via GitHub pull request.
**Catch:** Only for JavaScript-related projects/pages hosted on GitHub Pages.

### 6. DigitalPlat (dpdns.org)
**Link:** https://dpdns.org
**You get:** Free subdomains (e.g. `yourname.dpdns.org`) with a proper DNS dashboard. Currently the most active Freenom replacement.
**Catch:** It's a subdomain, not your own TLD — and free subdomain programs can change terms, so don't build a business on one.

### 7. DuckDNS
**Link:** https://www.duckdns.org
**You get:** `yourname.duckdns.org` with dynamic DNS — perfect for home servers, Raspberry Pi, self-hosting.
**Catch:** Subdomain only; best for personal/dev use, not public brands.

### 8. FreeDNS
**Link:** https://freedns.afraid.org
**You get:** Free subdomains across thousands of shared domains, plus free DNS hosting for a domain you already own.
**Catch:** Shared domains vary in quality — pick a clean, old one.

## ☠️ Dead / Avoid (old posts still recommend these!)

| Service | Status |
|---|---|
| **Freenom** (.tk, .ml, .ga, .cf, .gq) | Free tier **dead**. Paused registrations in March 2023 after Meta sued them; ICANN terminated their accreditation; they exited the free-domain business in Feb 2024 and ~12.6 million free domains stopped resolving. The site now sells .tk/.cf/.gq for ~$10/year — **not free any more**. |
| **us.kg / xx.kg** (DigitalPlat) | New registrations **paused** after abuse problems. Use their dpdns.org instead. |
| **qzz.io** | Moved to **paid-only**. |

## 💡 Tips

- **Free ≠ forever.** Freenom's collapse killed millions of sites overnight. Use free domains for side projects, portfolios and experiments — buy a real domain (~₹800–1,000/year for a .com) for anything that earns money.
- **Student?** The GitHub Student Pack is the single best deal here: real domains (.me / .tech / Name.com picks) for free.
- **Need it today?** is-a.dev and dpdns.org are the fastest. EU.org is the most "permanent" but the slowest.
- Always keep your site easy to migrate (your own code + backups), so a dead free domain never kills your project.
